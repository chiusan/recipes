Candy Bar Clusters

Description

These candy bar date clusters are a quick, no-bake treat made with Medjool dates, peanut butter, chocolate, and peanuts. They taste like a candy bar but come together in minutes with just a handful of ingredients and no added sugar.

Ingredients

  • 12 medjool dates, pitted
  • 1/2 cup peanut butter
  • ½ cup semisweet chocolate chips
  • 1 teaspoon coconut oil
  • 1/4 cup crushed peanuts
  • 1/4 teaspoon sea salt
