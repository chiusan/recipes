Dump cake

Description

In this cookies and cream dump cake, chocolate sandwich cookies, sweetened condensed milk, whipped topping, and devil’s food cake mix are layered into one irresistibly gooey dessert. Serve warm with an extra dollop of whipped topping.

Ingredients

  • cooking spray
  • 32 cream-filled chocolate sandwich cookies (such as Oreos), divided
  • 1 (14-ounce) can sweetened condensed milk
  • 1 (8-ounce) container frozen whipped topping, thawed, plus more for serving
  • 1 (15.25-ounce) package devil’s food cake mix
  • 3/4 cup salted butter, thinly sliced
  • 1/2 cup semisweet chocolate chips
